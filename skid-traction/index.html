STATIC FRICTION
The tyre is rolling without slipping. Static friction supplies the braking force.
Friction needed 2.2 kN
Maximum static friction 6.5 kN
Kinetic friction 5.0 kN
Static friction can take any value from zero up to its maximum. Kinetic friction applies only once sliding begins.
Static friction: Fs ≤ μsN
Maximum static: Fs,max = μsN
Kinetic friction: Fk = μkN
The point of tyre in contact with the road is not sliding.
Static does not mean stationary

The car and wheel are moving, but the tyre does not slide across the road at the point of contact. The friction is therefore static.

If the braking demand becomes greater than the maximum possible static friction, the tyre begins to slide. The friction then becomes kinetic.

Static friction increases as needed up to a maximum. Once sliding begins, kinetic friction is usually smaller.
