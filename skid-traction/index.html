Skid & Traction

Explore how static friction keeps a moving car on a curved road, and what happens when grip is lost.

STATIC FRICTION
The tyres are gripping the road. Static friction provides the centripetal force.
Friction required 2.9 kN
Maximum static friction 6.2 kN
Required: F = mv²/r
Static limit: Fs,max = μsmg
While skidding: Fk = μkmg
The inward friction force supplies the centripetal force while the tyres grip the road.
Static does not mean stationary

A rolling tyre can experience static friction because the tyre and road are not sliding relative to each other at the point of contact.

Once the tyre begins to slide across the road surface, the friction becomes kinetic.

The model uses a fixed 50 m bend radius, a 1000 kg car, and g = 10 m/s². Friction coefficients are simplified teaching values.
