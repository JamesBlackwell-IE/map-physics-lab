Separate the different motions

Spin is the rapid rotation of the projectile about its own longitudinal axis.

Precession causes that longitudinal axis to sweep around the direction of travel. In the end-on view, this appears as the blue axis rotating around the centre.

Nutation slightly changes the angular deviation during precession. This makes the axis tip move inward and outward, producing a rosette-like trace rather than a simple circle.

In the unstable mode, the angular deviation grows until nose-forward stability is lost and the projectile begins to tumble.

Spin is rotation about the projectile axis. Precession and nutation describe motion of that axis relative to the direction of travel.
