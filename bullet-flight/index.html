How to read the animation

Spin is the small red motion around the projectile’s own axis.

Precession makes the projectile axis sweep around the flight direction, producing full loops.

Nutation slightly changes the loop size, producing a rosette-like pattern rather than a simple repeated orbit.

A stable bullet can spin, precess and nutate at the same time. The key issue is whether that motion stays bounded or grows until tumbling occurs.
This is a conceptual teaching animation rather than a quantitative ballistics model. Motion is exaggerated for clarity.
