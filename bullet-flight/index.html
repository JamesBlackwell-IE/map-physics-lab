Stable flight can still involve angular motion

A stable spinning projectile does not need to point perfectly along its direction of travel. Small yaw, precession and nutation can remain bounded.

In the end-on view, precession appears as the projectile axis sweeping around the direction of travel. Nutation causes the distance from the centre to oscillate slightly.

In the unstable mode, the angular deviation progressively increases. Once nose-forward stability is lost, the projectile begins continuous end-over-end tumbling.

Yaw, precession and nutation can occur during stable flight. The important distinction is whether the angular motion remains bounded or grows.
This is a conceptual teaching animation rather than a quantitative external-ballistics model. The rates and angular motion are deliberately exaggerated for clarity.
