The dashed centreline is the direction of travel. The projectile axis may remain close to it, oscillate more widely, or eventually lose nose-forward stability.
Stable motion is not perfectly straight

A stable spinning projectile can still yaw, precess and nutate while remaining approximately aligned with its direction of travel.

In the unstable mode, the angular motion grows with time. Eventually the projectile can no longer remain nose-forward and begins to tumble.

Yaw and precession do not automatically mean instability. Instability is characterised by angular motion that grows rather than remaining bounded.
This is a conceptual teaching model. Angular motion is exaggerated and slowed for clarity and does not represent a specific projectile, calibre or rifling system.
