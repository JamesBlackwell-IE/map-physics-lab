Teaching model: 16 equally spaced point emitters, 1 mm apart, at 0.75 MHz. Sound speed 1540 m/s. Thin wavefront arcs show every second element for clarity. Wavefronts and the pulse envelope are shown in slow motion; time is in microseconds (µs). The intensity map uses coherent addition of ideal equal-amplitude waves, with no attenuation, geometric spreading or element directivity. It is a schematic transmit field, not an ultrasound image. Real transducers contain many elements and use more complex beamforming. Focusing here is on-axis.
